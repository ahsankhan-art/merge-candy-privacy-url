Privacy Policy Terms of Use

Merge Candy respects your privacy.

  • No account is needed to play. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • Game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, cosmetics and settings) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Anonymous gameplay analytics (for example "level 12 completed") help us balance the game. They contain no information that identifies you or your device, and they can be switched off in Settings.
  • Ads: the free version shows ads between levels and optional reward videos. Video ads are skippable — you can skip an ad between levels, and reward videos can be closed at any time (closing one early simply means no reward). Ads are requested as non-personalized and child-directed where required, so no advertising profile is built from your play. Buying "Remove Ads" removes the ads between levels; reward videos stay available and always optional.
  • Our ad partner may process a limited amount of technical data (such as device type, coarse country and an ad identifier where the platform allows it) to deliver and measure ads. Non-personalized ads are requested by default.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see or store your payment details.
  • Children: we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases and external links are behind a parental gate.
  • Contact: see Support in Settings, or write to the address at the bottom of this page.
